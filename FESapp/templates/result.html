The check that keeps every dose safe

Before a dose is given, the system predicts how much of the drug will build up in each of 13 organs over the next two days, and compares it with a safe limit for that organ. If any organ would go over its limit, the dose is lowered first. Each chart below is one organ; the red dashed line is its safe limit, and a line that stays below it means that dose was safe for that organ.

Drug levels are in the model's own units. They are predicted by a physiologically based pharmacokinetic (PBPK) model that follows the drug through the whole body.
