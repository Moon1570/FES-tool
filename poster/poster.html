Decision Support System
for Cancer Chemotherapy

Decide the right dose, before side effects appear.
What the clinician sees
The whole course of treatment is laid out before the first dose — how the tumour is expected to respond, and how close each dose comes to every organ's safe limit. Here 4 of 9 doses were lowered in advance, because the lung would otherwise have been pushed past its limit.

Problem

Chemotherapy has to shrink the tumour without harming healthy organs — but the dose is usually lowered only after the patient already has side effects.

Solution

Before a dose is given, the clinician can see what it is expected to do to the tumour and to each major organ — and which organ is limiting it.

Impact

The full course can be reviewed and alternatives compared before treatment starts, so the dose is decided in advance rather than in reaction.
