Problem

Chemotherapy has to shrink the tumour without harming healthy organs — but the dose is usually lowered only after the patient already has side effects.

Solution

Before a dose is given, the clinician sees what it is expected to do to the tumour and to each major organ — and which organ is limiting it.

Impact

The full course is reviewed and alternatives compared before treatment starts, so the dose is decided in advance rather than in reaction.

Why it matters for doctors

Fewer calculation mistakes

Body size and every dose adjustment are worked out automatically, not by hand.

See the impact first

The tumour's response and the side effects are projected over the whole course.

13 organs checked

A dose that would push any organ past its safe limit is lowered before it is given.

Thinks the way a doctor does

Mimics how an oncologist reasons, and explains every dose in plain words.

Personalised to each patient

Adjusts for body size, tumour burden and organ tolerance.

Compare before deciding

Put schedules side by side and choose the best balance for the patient.
